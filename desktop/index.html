Help & settings

Mirror your iPhone

Choose Mirror on the iPhone, scan the PC’s mirroring code, connect, then approve Share Screen. Use the viewer’s orientation and fullscreen controls to adjust the display.

Connect to this PC remotely

Choose Remote access, enable it, then Add iPhone. Scan that separate code in My PC on your iPhone. After authorization, use Connect on the saved PC—no repeat scan needed.

Privacy & connection

Screen video, app audio and remote input pass through your configured LiveKit service with encrypted transport. Application-level end-to-end encryption is not enabled. ScreenLink does not save recordings. The phone stores a device credential in Keychain; this PC stores its hash.

Remote access starts ScreenLink at sign-in and keeps the PC awake while enabled. Disable & revoke access removes the startup entry and saved authorizations. The PC must remain on and signed in.

Having trouble?

Check both devices have internet access and the PC app is running. Try Connect again. For a long trip, ensure the iPhone app’s Apple signing has not expired.
