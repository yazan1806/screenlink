## Description

Your devices, connected.

Use your own Windows PC from your iPhone, or bring your iPhone screen to the PC. ScreenLink keeps the two tasks separate, with clear setup steps and familiar controls.

YOUR PC, WHEREVER YOU ARE
Authorize your iPhone once, then connect to the saved PC with Face ID or your device passcode. Move the mouse, click, scroll, drag and type. Rotate your phone for fullscreen, and choose Fit or Fill to adjust the desktop view.

YOUR IPHONE, ON A BIGGER SCREEN
Scan a private mirroring code on the PC and approve screen sharing. See your iPhone screen and play supported app audio through the PC.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
Remote access requires explicit device authorization. Remove the saved PC on your iPhone or revoke devices from Windows. Screen sharing begins with Apple's system approval. ScreenLink itself does not save recordings.

Requires the ScreenLink Windows companion, a configured connection service, internet access and iOS 27 or later. The PC must remain on, signed in and running ScreenLink. Some protected apps may withhold video or audio. Network conditions and iOS determine actual performance. Media transport is encrypted; application-level end-to-end encryption is not enabled. Service availability and plan limits apply.
